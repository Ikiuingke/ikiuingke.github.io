[[file:index.org][Vinyl Finder]] is a free search engine focusing on [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Record_collecting][vinyl records]] sold by local record stores.

* Record stores
- Diggers get the release they're looking for directly in your stocks.
- Any record store with a web-shop for vinyls can be listed in the search engine.

* Artists & labels
- Check for the worldwide availability of your releases, and find new stores to contact.
- Any release will be indexed, as long as a record store has the vinyl listed on their website.

* Diggers
- An other tool to try and find these missing tracks on wax.
- Quickly check worldwide and local availability of any vinyl record ever released.
